# Playtest session sheet

Copy the parts needed for this session. Fill the placeholders with the actual game information.

## Observer brief

- Decision and question — what could we change, and what behavior would inform the choice?
- Setup — build or variant, relevant player familiarity, starting point, and any information normally available at this point.
- Count as independent success — observable event, clock start, and cutoff. For a simple choice, start with a 10-second observation window when the options are usable. Record the outcome in that window and the final choice separately.
- Intervene when — a preselected point or an explicit request that ends the unassisted attempt. Record all help and any deviation.
- If comparing versions — assign and record the exposure order. Use first exposures for a first-time discovery comparison.
- End with — one or two neutral follow-ups such as “What did you expect would happen?” or “What made you try that?”

## Words to say to the player

“We’re trying out the game. Please [describe the goal or section as the player would encounter it]. Let me know when you think you’re done.”

Use this as a starting point. Keep the comparison hypothesis and the expected route in the observer brief.

For the outcome, record independent, assisted, timed out, abandoned, or missing. Record any gap between where a player says they got stuck and what the observer saw. For a timed-out attempt, record the cutoff rather than a fabricated completion time.

## After the sessions

Summarize the evidence for the main decision. Record the exact numerator and denominator for each version, one concrete example, a plausible alternative explanation and the next check. Use anonymous labels such as P01.

## Method references

- [Games User Research on designing a study](https://gamesuserresearch.com/designing-a-games-user-research-study/) discusses matching tasks to objectives and preserving the knowledge players would have in the game.
- [GOV.UK on moderated usability testing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing) covers neutral tasks, consistent sessions and follow-up questions.
