# Supplied pose and expression references

This table records seven gesture and camera studies from the original project.
Use the descriptions to select or brief an authorized reference. Apply the
assigned pose or expression role to a fictional character.

| Study ID | Study | Useful adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — lateral lean | Shoulder/hip offset, diagonal torso, supported stance and a purposeful hand | An adult character's informal greeting or teasing pause; preserve their own clothes |
| 02 — forward lean | Forward inclination, slight torso rotation, broad support and asymmetric leg directions | A confident approach or attentive response; do not copy the model's likeness |
| 03 — compact crouch | Compressed silhouette, folded legs, arm-to-knee relationship and a face-supporting gesture | A low seated/crouched pause adapted to the character's shoes and joint range; costume not required |
| 04 — foreground perspective | Very strong near/far scale, seated support and foreshortened leg/foot | Dramatic camera study; avoid disconnected limbs or arbitrary oversized anatomy |
| 05 — close high viewpoint | Lean toward lens, high viewpoint, facial proximity and visible hand support | A playful approach to the viewer; preserve fictional identity and deliberate coverage |
| 06 — gesture toward viewer | High camera, near head/far body and active hand gestures | Curious or animated interaction; adapt proportions rather than stretching a neutral pose |
| 07 — asymmetric expression | Wink, uneven mouth, cheek/eye asymmetry and candid attitude | Distinct facial acting appropriate to the character; cap, text and face are not identity references |
