How the model works

The example assumes earnings are constant until the first purchase. With 10 coins, a 70-coin price and 12 coins per minute, the wait is 5 minutes.

Limits and what to verify

In a real game, record the time between rewards, competing purchases and any access rules. Check the calculation against a known game session before tuning.
