# Ink Punk — human calibration after 50 practice images

Received 2026-11-18. This refines 1.5; it does not replace the cast contract.
The observations summarize 50 user ratings and the accompanying review.
Ratings reflect the owner's taste, not universal beauty or blanket approval of
anatomy, props, identities and every surface in an image.

## Appeal is part of the job

Correct ink alone does not satisfy this direction. A new adult fashion character
needs a memorable design idea, personality and visible sensual appeal appropriate
to the requested moment. Judge the whole look at delivery size: face/gaze,
garment construction, silhouette and bodily acting together. Plainness is not
made successful by rendering accuracy alone (01,05,06). Keep male characters
distinctive too; this does not require a fixed flirtatious expression.

[07, closed gothic look](../assets/practice-50/07-long-face-gothic.png)
and [19, Lisa's pajama moment](../assets/practice-50/19-lisa-feather.png)
both received10/10. Closed clothing can carry allure through fit, long lines,
gentle acting and expression; skin area is not a success metric. These examples
govern appeal, not a mandatory garment formula or a new character identity.

  the owner rejected the over-thick contour. Bold ink means hierarchy and

## Delivery review is a gate

User rejected02,08,25 for perceived structural failures. Review torso-to-pelvis
rotation, support and each leg hip–thigh–knee–shin–ankle–foot, as well as both
full arm chains. In a complex pose, cloth folds cannot stand in for a coherent
underlying construction. Occlusion alone is not a defect; unresolved broken or
unreadable connections must not be marked ready just because no extra limb is
obvious. Fix them or keep the output internal with a rejected/needs-correction
status. Raw teaching anti-examples may be shown only as explicitly labelled
examples within an agreed exercise, not counted silently as ready work to rate.

Check physical contact separately from pretty hands: screwdriver/temple17,
wrench/fastener22, fabric/buckle25. Verify the working tip or jaw, contact
surface, overlap order and force direction. A pleasing face or high score
does not make an incorrect prop interaction acceptable.

Independent review did not establish every exact joint break reported by the
owner; several joints are hidden. Preserve that uncertainty rather than
inventing diagnostic anatomy. The rejected delivery status remains: the owner
could not read the action as physically convincing. Clear construction is the
production requirement.

## Scope of remaining decisions

Dasha's redesign was suggested, but no replacement design was chosen. Preserve
her profile until a separate design direction is developed and adopted. Scores
for Lisa do not license making every new heroine Lisa. 24 was described as
outside the style, but the exact cause was not isolated; do not ban chess,
monochrome or quiet tension because of that single reaction.
