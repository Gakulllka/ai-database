# Accepted practice: stronger adult appeal, construction defects excluded

On 2026-11-18, after the second five-character/five-CG study, the owner said:
«Это уже сильно лучше, фиксируем наработки, но ты стал чаще ломать сами арты».
The same message identifies the broken shirt, third arm and pool ladder/action.
The full feedback is recorded in the original project archive.

## What is adopted

The artistic progress is accepted: adult sexual appeal reads in the complete
look, coordinated through body rhythm, particular expression and attention,
garment cut/fit/drape and selected material contrast. This works with covered
fashion as well as homewear or swimwear. Preserve the moderate tapered ink,
connected dark shapes and crisp cel steps; don't replace them with glossy
skin or soft photographic painting. Accepted Lisa rendering remains authoritative;
these are complementary examples, not a new obligatory finish.

Inspect the two actual PNGs according to these limited roles:

- [Selina prototype](../assets/practice-v152-selina.png): a covered plum suit,
  shaped waist, long graphic rhythm and personal over-shoulder attention.
- [Agata prototype](../assets/practice-v152-agata.png): high-neck blouse, fitted
  waistcoat and long skirt; allure through fitted/flowing contrast, silhouette
  and an inviting gesture without relying on more exposed skin.

Neither example prescribes its identity, face proportions, costume, palette,
pose or materials for every new woman. They are still study characters, not a
permanent cast or mandatory anatomy templates. Broad smiles, concentrated
attention and different clothing families remain available. Do not collapse
the cast into their related face geometry. The owner's screenshot remains an
appeal-intensity example, explicitly not a rendering benchmark.

## What is excluded and why the review changed

The original02 Taya shirt combined a seated collar/shoulder with a slipped sleeve;
the same state carried into07. The original06 Selina CG had a third central
arm-shaped sleeve branch. Original10 had a nonfunctional ladder and posed to
the side instead of entering the water. These are rejected regions, not accepted
style properties. Prior positive structural verdicts for them are withdrawn.
The first pool repair also left a floating far rail and is not a positive example.

Keep allure and construction as separate findings. Two attractive hands don't
prove there are two arms; an appealing outfit doesn't validate its seams; a hand
on a rail doesn't validate its mounting. The concrete branch, garment, fixture
and action checks are in [posing-and-cg.md](../../../../skills/ink-punk/references/posing-and-cg.md).

Repairs preserve the accepted artistic direction but do not create a separate
human approval or numeric rating. A successful PNG check proves file properties
only. Keep the original failures and correction history in the study instead of
silently overwriting evidence.
