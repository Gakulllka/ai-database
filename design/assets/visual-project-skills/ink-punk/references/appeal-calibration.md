## Exact reference role

This is a calibration of the intended strength of adult sexual appeal and
how face, figure, costume and acting work together. It does not replace
Ink Punk's approved ink/cel anchors. Transfer the relevant design relationships,
not these characters, their proportions, composition or painted finish.

For the adult female fashion branch, the requested result is clearly sexy,
flirtatious or sensually magnetic as appropriate to the individual and scene.
A merely pleasant, tidy or distinctive person does not automatically meet it.
Use the actual whole-image impression; technical validity and a list of sexy
accessories do not prove success.

## Visible mechanisms to translate

- **Covered outfits:** the blue fitted suit and yellow long-sleeved costume
  still expose a readable bodily rhythm through fit, torso rotation, waist
  shaping, garment tension and material response. Coverage can remain high.
  For a looser costume, use deliberate fitted/loose relationships and drape;
  do not make every garment skin-tight.
- **Face and acting:** eyelids, eyebrows, lips, head tilt, gaze direction and
  proximity jointly create a particular response. The screenshot includes
  both knowing looks and an open smile. A broad smile is not inherently
  unsexy; a half-lidded smirk is not a universal solution.
- **Whole figure:** shoulders, ribcage, waist and pelvis form an intentional
  gesture; leaning, twisting and bending have readable support. Hands,
  body direction and attention relate to the same moment. More complicated
  anatomy is not automatically more sensual, and hidden joints remain uncertain.
- **Fashion and materials:** cut, neckline, layering, hosiery, soft fabric,
  lace or a fitted suit can frame different parts of the figure. The useful
  contrast may be a dark sleeve against light skin or matte fabric against
  a selected highlight. Not every design needs lingerie, a cleavage, all the
  materials, or the same shoulder/hip gesture.

These are mechanisms and examples, not a checklist to accumulate in each image.
Before generation, decide where this character's allure will be visibly carried:
body rhythm, expression/attention and a concrete costume relationship. Different
personalities may be playful, cool, bold, warm or reserved.

## What stays with Ink Punk and the current brief

Keep the established moderate tapered ink, connected near-black shapes,
one or two crisp cel-shadow steps and selective texture. Do not import the
screenshot's soft painted gradients, skin gloss or detailed rendering as the
new style. Keep the specified clearly adult 18–25 cast, fair/pale complexion
and slim/very lean baseline; do not copy exaggerated bust/hip volume or make
different heroines share one face/body. The screenshot does not establish
the identities or ages of its individual characters.

The bed arrangement, central companion, group intimacy, cosplay roles, weapons,
medical symbols, screen arrows, borders and player controls are not task
requirements. A new sprite remains on native alpha. Named characters keep
their own identity/outfit contract unless a requested redesign releases it.
